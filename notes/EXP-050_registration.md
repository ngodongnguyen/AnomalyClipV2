### EXP-050 — Does zoom augmentation teach a centre prior, and does the extent-conditioned pixel prompt carry it? (H039; diagnostic, no training)

- **Hypothesis / status:** H039 (a: source geometry; b: extent axis) / PLANNED (drafted 2026-10-15 before any EXP-050 code ran on real data; the user must confirm the rule before the first run; do not change it after seeing numbers). Diagnostic only: no training, no target labels except to SCORE (Part B), no method claim.
- **Motivation (observations, not mechanisms):** EXP-048/049 showed a PE-mediated position response whose size is ordered CTRL (PE-recentre gain +0.033 to +0.042) < ZO (+0.047 to +0.058) < ECP (+0.066 to +0.120), and an image-recentre gain that is near zero for CTRL but clear for ZO and ECP. Reading of `dataset.py` `_sample_crop_box` (2026-10-15): the zoom crop is square, must contain the whole anomaly bounding box and makes the anomaly fill 10-45% of its area; its corner is uniform over the admissible range, so the anomaly centroid can never lie closer to the crop edge than half of the bounding box. A large anomaly in a zoomed crop is therefore pushed toward the crop centre, and ECP pairs exactly these crops with large extent labels z (target 0.10-0.45 gives z about 0.80 to 1.56; the deployed z_pix = 1.6 lies at the large end). H039: (a) the training zoom moves MVTec anomalies toward the frame centre and/or ties large z to central position more than the native frames do; (b) in the frozen ECP, the PE position response is carried by the pixel prompt's z, so it shrinks at a small-extent prompt (z_pix = -0.69).
- **Part A (CPU, no model, no medical data):**
  - Data: MVTec `meta.json` **test** anomalous images with masks (the prompt-training source; EXP-040 audit), categories in the EXP-040 order; masks thresholded at > 0 as `Dataset.__getitem__`; images whose mask size differs from the image are skipped before any random draw (as EXP-040).
  - Simulation: the exact training crop code (`_sample_crop_box` compiled from the source of `dataset.py`, its SHA-256 recorded), `random.seed(111)`, image outer and epoch inner, 15 epochs, attempt with probability 0.5, target (0.10, 0.45); a failed attempt keeps the native frame. **Parity gate (STOP otherwise):** the attempt / success counts must equal the EXP-040 audit (9,407 / 8,768). This simulates the recipe; it cannot reproduce the historical DataLoader draws.
  - Per visit: z = `area_to_z`(anomaly area fraction of the frame) and c = centroid distance from the frame centre, each axis divided by its own half-side (= `zpos_stats.centroid_offset` on a square frame; the frame is resized to a square at training).
  - Statistics (image-cluster bootstrap, 2000 resamples, seed 0): **A1** = mean over images of (mean c over that image's zoomed visits - its native c), images with no zoomed visit excluded. **A2** = Spearman rho(z, c) over all visits (the training mixture) - rho(z, c) over the native frames (one row per image).
  - Labels: A1 CENTRALISES if A1 <= -0.05 and CI upper bound < 0; NO_CENTRALISATION if A1 >= -0.02. A2 COUPLES if A2 <= -0.10 and CI upper bound < 0; NO_COUPLING if A2 >= -0.03. Otherwise UNCLEAR. Values rounded to 1e-10 before comparing.
  - **Part A verdict:** SUPPORTED if A1 CENTRALISES or A2 COUPLES; NOT SUPPORTED if A1 NO_CENTRALISATION and A2 NO_COUPLING; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE; INCOMPLETE if no zoomed visit exists or the parity gate fails.
  - Report-only: mean c by z bin for native frames and zoomed visits; A1 per category; rho of the zoomed visits alone.
- **Part B (GPU, forward only, frozen ECP):**
  - Checkpoints: matched ECP seeds 111 (`checkpoints/EXP-012-matched/ecp_extent/checkpoints/epoch_15.pth`), 222, 333 (`checkpoints/matched_ecp_s{222,333}/epoch_15.pth`).
  - Run `analyze_pe_shift.py` UNCHANGED (EXP-048 protocol: peripheral tercile from the EXP-033 offset c, PE_REC, PE_RAND, IMG_REC, IMG_RAND, PE_FLAT, common valid region, per-image pixel AUROC, sigma 4, layer 24, 518 px) with `--z_pix -0.69`, `--no_central`, `--pooled_parity off`, outputs `results/EXP-050/zlow/<tag>/<set>/`. The z_pix = 1.6 arm is NOT rerun: it is the recorded EXP-048 (seed 111) and EXP-049 (seeds 222/333) per-image CSVs.
  - Pairing: peripheral images kept in both runs; the recentre and random PE shifts depend only on the mask and the image id, and the collector STOPs if they differ between the two runs for any paired image.
  - Statistic per checkpoint and set: G = PE_REC - PE_RAND per image (the direction-specific PE response, as EXP-048's G); G_hi at z 1.6, G_lo at z -0.69; Q = mean G_lo / mean G_hi; dG = mean (G_hi - G_lo) with a paired image-bootstrap 95% CI (2000, seed 0).
  - Set label: COUPLED if mean G_hi > 0, Q <= 0.50 and the dG CI lower bound > 0; NOT if mean G_hi > 0 and Q >= 0.80; INVALID if fewer than 3 paired images or mean G_hi <= 0; otherwise MIXED.
  - Seed verdict over the decision sets ClinicDB, ColonDB, Endo: fewer than 2 valid sets INCONCLUSIVE; >= 2 COUPLED Z-COUPLED; >= 2 NOT NOT Z-COUPLED; otherwise MIXED. **Part B verdict:** >= 2 of 3 seeds Z-COUPLED -> Z-COUPLED; >= 2 of 3 NOT Z-COUPLED -> NOT Z-COUPLED; otherwise MIXED; a missing CSV -> INCOMPLETE.
  - Report-only: SHAM per-image AUROC at both z, D_PE (PE_REC - SHAM) at both z, CTRL G from EXP-049 as the no-zoom reference, Kvasir and TN3K if run (`EXTRA_SETS`).
- **What each outcome authorises (fixed now):** Part A SUPPORTED authorises registering Step 2, a one-seed training pilot of a position-debiased zoom against the matched ECP with its own pre-registered rule (peripheral-tercile gain, no central-tercile loss, pooled PRO guard). Part A NOT SUPPORTED or INCONCLUSIVE: no position-debiased zoom training; the PE finding stays an analysis result. Part B changes only the paper's wording: Z-COUPLED allows "the extent-conditioned pixel prompt carries the position prior"; NOT Z-COUPLED or MIXED: the prior is not attributed to the extent axis. Neither part is a method claim.
- **Reading limits (written before the run):** Part A simulates the training recipe, not the historical random draws; c and z are computed on masks, not on what the model sees; a SUPPORTED premise is necessary, not sufficient, for the zoom to cause the PE response. Part B: z_pix = -0.69 changes the whole pixel map (its SHAM AUROC is lower, EXP-026 z sweep), so a smaller G may partly reflect a weaker map rather than a missing position prior; SHAM and D_PE at both z are printed to expose this, but the rule does not correct for it. The z_pix = 1.6 CSVs come from EXP-048/049 code; EXP-049 changes did not alter EXP-048 outputs (pe_shift_stats additions only). One recipe, MVTec source, development sets (D027).
- **Known design difficulty for Step 2 (not part of this rule):** a lesion that must lie entirely inside a square crop cannot have its centroid near the crop edge, so an exactly position-uniform zoom is impossible for large lesions without cutting the lesion (which changes the extent label) or padding (which hurt in EXP-037). Step 2 must state its choice before training.
- **Cost:** Part A CPU, minutes (about 1,250 masks, 15 visits each, 2,000 bootstrap resamples of a Spearman on about 19,000 visits). Part B: 6 forwards per peripheral image, 3 checkpoints x 3 decision sets, about the ECP share of EXP-049 restricted to three sets (estimate 45-60 min, unmeasured).
- **Code:** `centre_prior_stats.py` and `test_centre_prior_stats.py` (statistics, planted known-answer tests, the real `_sample_crop_box` compiled from `dataset.py`), `analyze_centre_prior.py` (`source`, `zreport`, `report`), `scripts/run_exp050.sh` (`check`, `smoke`, `source`, `zlow`, `report`). Local checks: all unit tests pass on CPU without torch; an end-to-end run on a synthetic MVTec tree and synthetic Part B CSVs completed (pipeline check only, numbers meaningless). Not tested locally: real MVTec masks, the EXP-040 parity, GPU forwards at z_pix -0.69.
